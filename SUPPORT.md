# Getting Support for CutoverCheck by JE Horizon

We provide comprehensive support for all users of **CutoverCheck by JE Horizon**.

## How to Get Help

- **Documentation & FAQs:** Visit our knowledge base at [https://jehorizon.com/help/cutovercheck/](https://jehorizon.com/help/cutovercheck/).
- **Direct Email Support:** Reach our engineering support team directly at [support@jehorizon.com](mailto:support@jehorizon.com). Please include your app version and Windows build number.
- **Bug Reports & Feedback:** If you encounter unexpected behavior, please file a ticket in our [GitHub Issues](../../issues).
